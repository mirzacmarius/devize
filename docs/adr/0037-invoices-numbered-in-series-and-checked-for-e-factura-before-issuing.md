# Invoices are numbered in series, checked for e-Factura before issuing, and never deleted once issued

A Deviz accepted by the client ends in invoices: the advance the Ofertă asked, each confirmed Situație de lucrări, and the rest once the works are done. Contractors made these in another program, retyping the client and the sums, and every invoice has had to reach ANAF's e-Factura system since 2024 (B2B) and 2025 (B2C), within five working days (OUG 89/2025), or the firm is fined. Marius's ask of 23 October 2026 to make Devize the best estimate app put invoicing in it, so a Deviz is billed where it was priced.

## Numbers come from the series, inside the mutation that issues

An invoice is a draft (`status: "draft"`) until it is issued. Issuing (`invoices.issue`) reads the series (`invoiceSeries`), takes its `nextNumber`, writes it on the invoice and moves the series on, all in one mutation, so two people issuing at once never get the same number and the numbers of a series have no gaps, as the law on fiscal documents expects (OMFP 2634/2015). A firm that set up no series gets `DVZ` starting at 1 the first time it issues; it can add its own series in Setări → Facturare, continue the numbering it had before Devize, and move the next number forward but never back. The issue date can't be in the future, nor before the last invoice of the series.

An issued invoice never changes and is never deleted, except with its firm (ADR 0050): a wrong one is reversed by a Storno (`kind: "storno"`, lines negated, `stornoOf` and `stornoedBy` linking the two), as Romanian practice and the Codul fiscal (art. 330) expect, and only an invoice that never left the firm (not sent to ANAF, unpaid) can be cancelled, keeping its number taken. Drafts are deleted freely, and a Deviz with an issued invoice can't be deleted.

## The client and the firm are copied onto the invoice

An invoice carries its own `seller` and `buyer` (`invoiceParty`): name, CUI or CNP, VAT registration, trade register, the address as street, city, county and postal code, and for the seller the IBAN, bank and share capital. They are copied when the draft is made, from the firm and its invoicing settings and from the client's card, and re-copied for the seller when issuing, so a later change to the client's card changes no issued invoice, and the XML of 2026 reads as the invoice did. A company client is looked up in ANAF's public register (`invoices/anaf.ts`, through the register client in `producers/anaf.ts`) when the draft is made, filling what the card lacks and saying whether it is registered for VAT; the person can ask again and let ANAF's record replace the name and office. The address is kept apart because the XML wants it so, and the previous invoice's corrected client carries over to the next one of the same Deviz.

## The XML is UBL 2.1 CIUS-RO 1.0.1, checked before issuing

`invoices/ubl.ts` writes the invoice as the UBL 2.1 Invoice that ANAF accepts, with `CustomizationID urn:cen.eu:en16931:2017#compliant#urn:efactura.mfinante.ro:CIUS-RO:1.0.1`, counties as ISO 3166-2 codes (`RO-CJ`, `RO-B`) and Bucharest's city as `SECTOR1`–`SECTOR6`, a person without CNP as thirteen zeros, VAT worked out per rate on the taxable amount of that rate (EN 16931 BR-CO-17), category `S`, `Z`, `E`, `AE` or `O` for a firm outside VAT with `VATEX-EU-O`, the advance deducted as negative lines, the Situație's period as `InvoicePeriod`, and the retention in the payment terms. `invoices/rules.ts` checks, with no database, what the XML would lack: missing names, streets, counties, sectors, CUIs, mixed VAT categories, a due date before the issue date, lines without quantity. An error stops issuing (`invalid("checks")`), so the firm fixes the invoice on its page rather than reading ANAF's rejection days later; a warning only tells. The same findings show on the page as the draft is shaped, and the person can post the XML to ANAF's public validator (`webservicesp.anaf.ro/prod/FCTEL/rest/validare/FACT1`, no credentials) from the page; the answer is kept on the invoice. The e-Factura deadline is five working days from the issue date, with Romania's legal holidays, Orthodox Easter included, in `invoices/deadline.ts`; the list and the e-Factura card show it, and flag an invoice still unsent after it.

Sending to ANAF's SPV is not in this step. It needs a qualified certificate registered in SPV and an ANAF OAuth application (client id and secret, callback) that only Marius can obtain; until then the firm downloads the XML and uploads it in SPV by hand. The `efactura` state on the invoice (`not_sent`, `uploading`, `processing`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `failed`, with the upload index and download id) is shaped for that step, so it adds a module and no migration.

## What is invoiced, and from where

The advance (`createAdvance`) is a share of the accepted Ofertă's value without VAT, at the Deviz's VAT rate, offered once per Deviz. The final invoice (`createFinal`) bills the accepted value and deducts every issued advance with a negative line that `settles` it. A Situație de lucrări is invoiced (`invoices/statements.ts`) once it is approved, as one line at the statement's value without VAT, with its period and the Garanție de bună execuție it keeps back shown after the total, through `readStatementForInvoice` in `progress/invoiceData.ts` (ADR 0035); one invoice per statement, and the statement keeps the rows. The invoice's PDF (`packages/forms/src/invoice.ts`) prints what art. 319 of the Codul fiscal asks and nothing it has to make up, with the series and number, both parties, the lines with VAT by rate, the totals, the mentions the firm set (TVA la încasare, its own footer) and that the invoice is valid without signature and stamp.

## Consequences

- Everything lives in `convex/invoices/`, `packages/forms/src/invoice.ts`, the pages `/invoices`, `/invoices/[id]` and `/settings/billing`, a card on the Deviz's page and a button on an approved Situație. Estimates gained only the delete rule and cascade; progress gained nothing.
- Money rounding follows the Deviz's `roundLei`; VAT is per rate, not per line, so an invoice's VAT can differ by a ban from the sum of its lines' VAT, as EN 16931 wants.
- Series numbers, the storno model and the VAT categories are fixed by law; later work (SPV sending, proforma invoices, recurring invoices) builds on these tables and keeps `issue` the only place a number is given.
- The super admin sets nothing for this step. SPV sending will need `ANAF_CLIENT_ID` and `ANAF_CLIENT_SECRET` on Convex and the firm's own OAuth consent.
