# Manufacturer outreach runs in the admin panel, with Claude, and never sends email

To seed the catalog, Devize asks Producători for the Norme de producător they already have. The first plan had a Grok bot research each Producător and draft its email outside Devize. On 21 October 2026 Marius chose to run this inside Devize instead, in an admin panel that only super admins can open, and with another AI provider. It uses Anthropic's Claude API: Claude researches a Producător with web search and web fetch, and reads the norm PDFs a Producător sends.

## Consequences

- Devize never sends outreach email. Research ends in a draft rendered from a fixed template, not written by the model, and a super admin approves it, sends it from their own mailbox and marks it sent. Each draft is checked against the rules for unsolicited business email (Legea 506/2004 and GDPR): one company at a time, a way to answer „stop”, no guessed addresses and a suppression list.
- Opening a draft in the email app or Gmail, or copying its text, approves it, since that is when a super admin decides to send it; the buttons work once the draft breaks no rule. Approve on its own stays in the queue, for sending later.
- Every email links to Devize's public privacy page, `/confidentialitate`, unless Admin → Setări names another one. The page tells the people we write to what GDPR art. 14 asks: where we found their address, why, on what basis, how to object and how long we keep it. Who answers for the data (the controller's name, registration, office and privacy address) is written in Admin → Setări, not in this public repository, and no draft that links the page can be approved before it names the controller and an address. The page promises that the addresses of people who never answered go within six months of the last email; nothing deletes them by itself yet.
- Every fact keeps the link it came from, and facts whose link the research never opened are dropped. An address counts as verified only once it is read on a page, such as the company's contact page.
- Imported norms are drafts: a super admin reviews each article against its page in the PDF before it is published. Norm documents and the data taken from them stay out of this repository, which is public.
- Devize publishes a Producător's norms only with its written agreement on record. The super admin notes on the norms document who agreed, when and where the agreement is, and nothing from that document is published until the note is there.
- Once a Producător sends its norms, the super admin drafts a thank-you to the address they came from, the same way: a fixed template, approved and sent from their own mailbox. It carries a claim link for that address (ADR 0018), says how much of the norms we digitized and asks for the written agreement unless it's on record. A draft whose recipient no longer matches its link, or whose profile was claimed meanwhile, can't be approved, and nobody on the suppression list gets a link.
- A Producător that a firm claimed, or whose norms, products or systems are in the catalog, can't be deleted, only excluded.
- The API key lives only in the Convex deployment's environment (`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, and `AI_MODEL` to change the model). Each job's usage and list-price cost is kept on `aiJobs`, failed jobs included.
