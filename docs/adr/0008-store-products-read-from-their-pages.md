# Store products are read from their pages, one link at a time

ADR 0003 settled that, until a retailer gives Devize a feed, a price is read from the retailer's site only for a product a Deviz needs. This ADR says how. A Constructor pastes the link to a store's product page for a material of a Deviz; Devize reads that one page and keeps the store product as the firm's choice for that material, with its price. Store pages differ and change often, so Devize reads only the structured data shops publish for search engines: schema.org `Product` in JSON-LD, else the Open Graph and microdata tags. It knows no store's layout.

## Consequences

- Devize reads as `DevizeBot`, with its site's address in the user agent, and only pages its robots.txt allows (RFC 9309, read again daily; when robots.txt can't be read, neither is the page). A 401, 403, 429 or 503 pauses reads of that store for an hour, or for its `Retry-After` up to a day. Devize never works around a refusal, a captcha or bot protection.
- One read per store every 4 seconds; a read that would wait more than 2 minutes is refused. At most 500 reads per store and 100 per firm a day. A page read in the last 12 hours answers from its Listare without reading the store again, and doesn't count. Reads are logged in `storeReads` and their pacing kept in `storeStates`, in the same transaction that accepts a read.
- A page read becomes one Listare per address with no firm, which every firm may use: the price with VAT (21 %), the title, EAN, stock and the date, with its history. When its EAN is a catalog product's, the Listare is linked to that product for everyone.
- What a firm buys is a Produs ales (`storeChoices`): at most one store product per material and store, with how many of the material's units one item sold holds, such as 25 for a 25 kg bag of a material counted in kg. Devize guesses it from the title and a person confirms or corrects it. Only that firm sees its choices.
- When a page can't be read, the Constructor types the price the store shows. It is a store Listare with the firm's id, so only that firm sees it.
- This changes the price order of ADR 0005 for materials: a product takes the firm's own price or a quotation, then its Produse alese, then public listings; a generic material takes the firm's own price, then its Produse alese, then public store listings linked to it. Among store prices the Deviz's preferred store comes first, else the lowest price per unit. A material no firm chose a store product for prices as before, so existing devize and their tests keep their prices.
- Brico Dépôt's Romanian stores became BRICO (Altex, 2025) and sell at bricostore.ro. The key stays `brico_depot`; the interface says BRICO.
- The reader was tested only on made-up pages, because the stores' sites can't be reached from the sandboxes Devize is built in. The first real reads will show which stores publish structured data, and a store that doesn't needs typed prices or a feed.
