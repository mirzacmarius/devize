# The authority's lists are filled in its own spreadsheet

Many contracting authorities publish a Licitație's Liste de cantități as an .xlsx and want it back with the bidder's prices, in its own rows and columns, beside or instead of F3. Typing a Deviz's prices into it by hand is slow and is where a price stops matching F1. So Devize writes the prices into the file the authority sent, in the browser like the other documents (ADR 0009), with `packages/forms/src/workbook`: it finds each Articol imported from the lists in the file, and writes numbers into the price cells of its row and of the list's Recapitulație, without changing anything else.

## Consequences

- The file is one of the Deviz's tender files uploaded with the import (ADR 0013), opened at once when it is the only .xlsx, or another one from the computer. It never goes to the server, and the filled copy is downloaded, not stored. Hidden sheets are neither read nor written, and an .xls is refused as on import.
- An Articol's row is the one with its `tenderRef` quantity and U.M., and its number, code or text on that row or the two above it. The positions are matched in the order the forms print them, and each sheet's quantities are looked for in its own column, so the same quantity in two lists, or twice in one, goes to the right row.
- The header over the positions names the price columns: a unit price and a value, or each kind's (materiale, manoperă, utilaj, transport). Rows a)–d) under a position get each kind's price, and the rows that close a list, from "Total" to "TOTAL cu TVA", its Recapitulație: each chapter's own, then the whole list's under a total general. When the header names no price column, the two beside the quantities are proposed. The person sees each sheet's columns and can choose others, or none.
- The prices are F3's: the tender's U.M. and quantity, with unit prices per kind rounded to bani. The list's Recapitulație adds the Cote it has a row for, with the Deviz's percentages, and the unit prices carry the others, CAM in the labour and the indirect costs and profit in every kind, so each sheet comes to the offer's value but for rounding. The person can also put them all in the unit prices.
- Only numbers are written, so the shared strings never change. A cell keeps its style, and a new one takes its row's or column's. A cell holding the authority's text, such as "se va completa", and the quantities' column are left alone. A formula stays, with the number written as the value it last showed, and the workbook is set to compute every formula when it opens; the archive's other parts go back byte for byte. Excel and LibreOffice then show the totals Devize computed, since the authority's formulas multiply and add the same numbers; a product they don't round can differ by a few bani.
- An authority's formula that works out a percentage in the Recapitulație, such as a VAT at an old rate, is computed again by Excel with its own numbers. Devize never changes a formula, so the sheet names those rows for the person to check.
- The sheet lists the rows that look like positions but have no Articol in the Deviz, to price by hand; the Deviz's positions the file doesn't have, since a tender may send several files; and the Articole that no list has, whose prices no file takes.
- Writing the archive takes fflate (MIT), which loads with the first file opened, like the PDF library. A file of more than 30 MB, a part declaring more than 64 MB unpacked, 160 MB in all, or more than 400,000 cells is refused.
