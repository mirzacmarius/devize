# Chosen store products are read daily, and sent devize are checked against them

A Deviz is priced on its date and then never changes (ADR 0005), but a firm still needs to know when the prices behind a Deviz it sent have moved: the client pays the price sent, so dearer materials come out of the firm's profit. Until now a store's price changed only when a firm happened to read that product's page again (ADR 0008). This ADR keeps the prices of the products firms actually buy fresh, and checks sent devize against them every morning.

## Consequences

- While at least one Firmă has a store product as a Produs ales, Devize reads its page again a day after the last read (`listings.refreshAfter`). A job runs every 5 minutes and starts at most one such read per store, through the same queue, limits and rules as on-demand reads (ADR 0008): robots.txt, `DevizeBot`, 4 seconds between reads of a store, a pause after a refusal. It skips a store whose queue is busy with firms' reads, and stops for the day once the store had 300 reads, so firms keep the rest of the 500. These reads have no firm, so no firm's daily limit pays for them.
- This extends ADR 0003's "when a Constructor adds it to a Deviz or asks for a refresh": choosing a store product is asking Devize to keep its price current. Devize still never crawls a catalog: it reads only pages firms chose, at most 288 a day per store. A product nobody chooses anymore stops being read; a page that is gone marks its Listare unavailable.
- Every morning at 04:00 UTC, Devize prices each Deviz that is sent or accepted, and whose status changed in the last 180 days, again at today's prices, like `estimates.checkUpdates`, keeping prices typed on the Deviz. One scheduled mutation per Deviz, so a large tender has a transaction of its own. When the total would change by at least the firm's threshold (`priceSettings.alertPercent`, 5 % unless the firm chose another, 0 to turn alerts off), it keeps one Alertă de preț per Deviz in `priceAlerts`; otherwise it removes it. An alert the check stopped renewing (the Deviz was deleted, went back to draft or got too old) is dropped after 36 hours.
- The alert never patches the Deviz: its totals, Recapitulație and `updatedAt` stay as the client saw them. It reports the total with VAT as sent and at today's prices, the direct costs both ways, and the profit left if the client pays the price sent: the Deviz's profit minus what the direct costs grew by. Indirect costs are taken as unchanged. It lists the five resources whose price change adds most to the direct costs.
- A dismissed alert stays hidden until the change moves by the threshold again. Changing the threshold, or asking for a check (at most once in 5 minutes), checks the firm's devize at once.
- The Prețuri page shows the alerts, the firm's chosen products with their latest price and its last change, and the threshold, which only a firm's admins change. The menu counts the alerts not dismissed.
