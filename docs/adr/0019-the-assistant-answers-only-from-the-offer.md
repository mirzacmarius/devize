# The assistant answers a client's questions only from their Ofertă

A client reading an Ofertă on their phone has questions, and most are about what it already says: what a price includes, which materials, how the Variante differ, what the indirect costs are. Waiting for the Firmă to answer those slows the decision, while a general chatbot would make up dates, discounts and guarantees that would bind the Firmă. So the Ofertă's page takes questions, an assistant answers them from the Ofertă as the link shows it, and whatever it can't answer goes to the Firmă, who replies on the same page.

## Consequences

- The assistant reads what `convex/offers/content.ts` builds for the link: the same fields the client sees, priced for the choice on their screen, with the Firmă's name, phone and email. No notes, resource prices or price sources reach the model. The prompt treats the Ofertă and the client's words as data, not instructions.
- It answers in Romanian, addressing the client as they address it, and gives figures only as the Ofertă has them. When the Ofertă doesn't say, or only the Firmă can decide (dates, discounts, changes, guarantees), it says so and the question goes to the Firmă. A failure, a refusal, or no answer within five minutes do the same, so every question has someone to answer it.
- Questions have their own table, `offerQuestions`, with the choice they were asked about, the assistant's answer and the Firmă's reply. The Firmă reads them on the Deviz's page and replies or changes its reply there; the client sees the reply on the link. Deleting the Deviz deletes them.
- A question waiting for the Firmă emails whoever made the link, if Resend is set up (as in ADR 0016): one email while a question of that link waits, so a client asking five things doesn't send five. The email failing doesn't fail the question.
- A question has at most 1 000 characters, and a link takes one at a time and 20 a day. The assistant answers at most 200 questions a day across a Firmă's links; past that they go to the Firmă until the next day. The Firmă can turn the assistant off for a link, when making it or later, and then every question goes to the Firmă.
- Each answer is an AI job (`aiJobs`, kind `offer_question`) on the Firmă, without a member, since the client has no account. The Ofertă is the prompt's first part and is cached, so a client's next questions read it from the cache; the last ten questions and the new one follow it. One call per question, with no thread to keep: the table is the conversation.
- The page says that the assistant answers only from the Ofertă and can be wrong, and that what counts is the Ofertă and what the client agrees with the Firmă. In the Firmă's preview of the link, the questions show but can't be asked.
