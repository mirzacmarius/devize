# Write our own catalog of base norms

Contractors price work from norms. The norm indicators most Romanian deviz software still ships date from 1981: many of their terms and materials, such as "var stins", are no longer used. Devize writes its own catalog of Norme de bază from scratch, with current terms, materials and units, and Producători add Norme de producător for their own products.

## Consequences

- No import of the 1981 indicators. When a Licitație's quantity lists still use their codes, the import matches each position to a current Normă instead.
- Every Normă is versioned. Changing one creates a new version, and devize keep the version they were priced with.
- ROCKWOOL is the first pilot Producător; their norms arrive by email, and they will be asked for an update later.
